I went to the [gradireland Graduate Careers Fair](https://gradireland.com/events/gradireland-graduate-careers-fair-2026) at the RDS this week. It was a good chance to meet employers face to face, hear about different career paths, and come away with a few ideas for what to do next.

I attended two talks. Jill Barrett presented a session on LinkedIn and networking, which got me thinking about how I present my experience and make connections. I also went to the student volunteering talk. It was a useful reminder that what we do outside the classroom can help us develop skills and find out what interests us.

The conversations with employers were pleasant. People were approachable and happy to answer questions, which made it easier to learn about the opportunities on offer. I also got a professional headshot taken, which was a nice bonus. The event was well organised, too: moving between the stands and talks felt straightforward, and I could make good use of my time there.

GeoJSON Three is an optimized reimplementation of the GeoJSON renderer I developed for Geom3D, my BSc thesis project. It uses float64 anchors and local vertex offsets to render features accurately at large projected or geocentric coordinates.

The library supports GeoJSON geometries, styling, picking, extrusion and editable points, lines and polygons. Its demos include a site plan, a precision comparison, large point sets, an editor and a viewer for your own files.

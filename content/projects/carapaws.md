A mobile dog-care application I am developing with a teammate for the Global Classroom module at TU Dublin during my Erasmus exchange. CaraPaws helps owners stay connected to their dogs while they are in a sitter's care, through photo updates and easy access to care information.

The prototype has separate owner and sitter experiences, with dog profiles, care lists and a chat-like updates view. I worked on simplifying the navigation so owners can reach their dog's updates immediately, while sitters can quickly choose a dog and share a photo. The interface uses React, Material UI and Capacitor to build for Android.
